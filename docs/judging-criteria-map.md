# Judging criteria → where Keynection answers them

> **给队员的中文摘要**：这一页是附录的第 1 页，也是路演讲稿的骨架。官网六项评审标准，每一项对应到原型的哪一页、评审路线的第几步、哪一份证据档案，以及我们还缺什么。数字只在这里和 `testing-overview.md` 写一次，其他地方引用。证据档案（普查、纠纷案例、App 评论、真机核对）放在团队资料夹 `evidence/`，交件时作为附录档案一起交，不在这个代码仓库里。

Six criteria, weights and wording from the official hackathon page (re-read 16 Oct 2026, unchanged). For each: where the prototype answers it, which step of the 3-minute judge route shows it, the evidence file behind it, and what is still missing.

Demo: https://keynection.onrender.com (shared password given privately; first open after a quiet spell takes about 40 s). The 3-minute route is the top of the Guide panel (top-right), one click per step.

The Command view counts **next-door pairs who know each other**, **days from keys to the first hello**, **pre-work photo coverage** and **concerns settled between neighbours**. | 3 | **Innovation & Differentiation** (20%) | "Originality of the idea and how it improves upon or differs from existing solutions." | (a) The **five homes beside and opposite** appear on day one, from the site plan, no directory, no township group. (c) **Noisy hours are planned around neighbours' private quiet times** by plain rules before the notice goes out, never saying whose. ## How the appendix is ordered

1. This page (criteria map).
2. `feature-status.md` — what is live, what is simulated and labelled, what is pilot-only.
3. `technical-summary.md` — architecture, state machines, permissions, known limitations.
4. `key-decisions.md` — why it is built this way.
5. `testing-overview.md` — the test layers and numbers.
6. `test-plan.md` — the manual plan, with the real-device table from `evidence/device-check/`.
7. `glossary.md` — terms in English and Chinese.
8. Evidence files: census, dispute cases, app reviews, device screenshots, route screenshots.
