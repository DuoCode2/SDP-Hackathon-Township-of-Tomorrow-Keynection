# Key decisions and why

> **给队员的中文摘要**：评审的「为什么」在这一页一次答完：为什么邻居是主角、为什么从交钥匙开始、为什么第一步不动 SDP App、为什么没有群组、为什么吵闹时段用规则不用 AI、为什么分流只看时间、为什么只有屋主能关单也没有暂停键、为什么承包商通行证收起来、为什么 AI 只放两处、为什么用数字钥匙、为什么三语介面四语阅读、为什么一套代码两种模式、为什么 SQLite 一个容器、为什么电脑视觉不用机器学习、为什么不连外网。每条写决定、理由、放弃了什么。

| # | Decision | Why | What we gave up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Neighbour connection is the hero; disputes are the safety net** | The team's own experience ("we don't even know the uncle opposite"); DOSM 2020: 13.2% of Selangor residents had no face-to-face neighbour contact in a month vs 6.5% nationally. Public evidence of renovation disputes is thin (28 incidents found nationwide, 2022–2026), so a dispute tool alone would overstate the problem | A louder "dispute" pitch |
| 2 | **Start on key day, through the welcome pack** | Handover is the one moment SDP meets every owner in person; a code in the pack costs SDP nothing new. Renovation follows handover, so the first notice is the natural first hello | Open sign-up (anyone could claim a unit) |
| 3 | **Step one changes nothing in the SDP App; step two is a module inside it** | SDP's 2026 priority is one integrated platform and the judges are SDP's digital team, so a separate app would be a step backwards; but a pilot should not wait for an app release. Elmina Valley Community, SDP's earlier separate app, scored 1.5–2.6 in the stores | Saying "the SDP App is optional" |
| 4 | **Five homes, no township group** | Group chats grow loud and are where disputes start (the i-Neighbour and GL Lifestyle+ reviews); a circle of five plus requests keeps every contact wanted and traceable | Reach beyond the circle without a request |
| 5 | **Noisy hours are planned by plain rules, not AI; quiet times stay private** | A rule can be explained to a neighbour and checked in court; it works offline; the neighbour never has to disclose a baby or a night shift. The owner always keeps at least 4 hours a day | Optimising for the renovating owner's convenience |
| 6 | **Triage looks only at time order; people decide; nothing pre-selected** | Time can rule a cause out (a crack photographed before the works) but never prove one; a tool that "finds the culprit" would be wrong and blamed. "Caused by the renovation" needs the other side's agreement; otherwise an SDP inspector | A smarter, less defensible verdict |
| 7 | **Only the owner closes a warranty ticket, and the 30-day clock never pauses** | The Housing Development Act gives the owner 24 months and the developer 30 days from written notice; there is no pause in the law, so the app has none. A rejected repair reopens the ticket with the clock unchanged | SDP's convenience of a pause |
| 8 | **Contractor passes and the guardhouse scan are built but hidden** | They manage workers, not neighbours; VYROX and i-Neighbour already do it well. Kept on the server for a mentor's question (`SHOW_CONTRACTOR_PASSES`) | A feature judges might recognise |
| 9 | **AI in two places only, always marked, works without it** | One sentence → notice fields saves the owner typing; words arriving in the reader's language is the one thing rules can't do in a four-language township. Everything else is rules. No key, no network, no answer: the same screens work by hand. Claude by design; Gemini's free tier for the fictional demo only, never a pilot | Chatbots, noise meters, face recognition at the gate, blockchain (all considered and rejected) |
| 10 | **Digital keys (passkeys), not passwords or SMS** | Residents already unlock their phones; nothing to remember or phish; keys are bound to the pilot's address. A lost phone is handled at the front desk with an ID check | Sign-in on a shared computer |
| 11 | **Three interface languages, four reading languages** | Screens in English, Malay and Chinese cover the team's ability to check them; what residents *receive* (quick replies, notices, paper, privacy notice) also comes in Tamil, because Kumar next door must get the notice he can read. Tamil needs a native speaker before a pilot | Tamil screens today |
| 12 | **One codebase, demo mode and pilot mode** | Judges need to switch roles and reset; residents need real sign-in and no fiction. One switch keeps the two honest and the tests shared; the shared demo starts over by itself between judges | Two separate builds |
| 13 | **SQLite and one container** | A pilot is one phase on one server; the simplest thing SDP IT can run; a backup is a file copy; no cloud account to approve first | Many townships on one database (not needed for a pilot) |
| 14 | **Computer vision by rule, not machine learning** | OpenCV aligns the two photos and boxes what darkened; it is explainable and needs no training data. We say "computer-vision comparison", never "AI scan" | Finding changes that brighten; robustness to strong shadows |
| 15 | **No network dependency in the demo** | Fonts, icons and QR codes are bundled; judges' rooms have bad Wi-Fi | A smaller bundle |
| 16 | **Honest labels on anything simulated** | A judge who finds one invented thing distrusts the rest. WhatsApp and the SDP App phones say "preview, nothing is sent" and "illustration"; the CIDB tick says "simulated check"; the demo's Procore number is marked demo | A slicker-looking demo |
| 17 | **Feature freeze since 10 Oct 2026; only deepening since 16 Oct** | The briefing warned that teams which try to solve everything solve nothing. Each change since then had to be visible on the 3-minute route, answer a judging criterion and be done and verified within a day | Several good ideas (notification bell, read ticks, multi-photo, dark mode…) |
